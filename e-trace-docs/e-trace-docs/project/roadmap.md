# Roadmap

{% hint style="info" %}
Dates and funding figures are **targets** for planning and may change.
{% endhint %}

### Phase 0 · Now: testnet MVP (done, Q3 2026)

* Multi-seller escrow with buyer confirm, refund and dispute, plus supervisor arbitration
* Embedded wallet with PIN, email OTP, 2FA and Turnstile; MetaMask login
* Paylater lending pool with fixed terms and profit sharing
* AI auto-settlement keeper and the On-Time Guarantee
* Disaster Radar with on-chain donation campaigns
* Public Transparency Explorer
* Seller dashboard, reports (Excel/PDF), sponsored home banners
* English and Bahasa Indonesia

### Phase 1 · Q4 2026 – Q1 2027: stabilize and onboard

Polish and stabilize the five core features (**e-commerce, donations, AI assistant, e-wallet, community savings**) and onboard the first sellers and communities.

* **Smart-contract audit.** Internal review first, then an external audit before any real money is involved.
* **Self-serve seller onboarding**, so anyone can open a store without asking the team.
* **Self-serve ad booking.** Advertisers book and pay for home-banner slots in TLKM, with an on-chain receipt and a public ad-revenue counter.
* **Courier API integrations**, so real tracking events feed the keeper instead of the simulated ones used on testnet.
* **Stablecoin support** at checkout alongside TLKM.

### Phase 2 · Q2 – Q3 2027: smarter AI, mainnet, expansion

* **AI assistant upgrade:** more personal financial recommendations, better product search with EVA
* More product categories and more donation and community partners
* Mainnet launch on BNB Smart Chain
* A price oracle and proper liquidation for Paylater
* Less reliance on a single arbiter: multisig or community arbitration for disputes
* Performance-based ads (per impression or per click)
* Mobile app
* Onboarding Indonesian SMEs and community groups (cooperatives, schools, mosques) for community wallets and donations

### Phase 3 · Q4 2027 onward: open transparency API

Open E-Trace's transparency infrastructure to third parties (**donation institutions, cooperatives, government agencies**) through a public API, so they can use escrow, on-chain receipts and the explorer outside the core marketplace. This is also where institutional partnerships become a revenue stream.
