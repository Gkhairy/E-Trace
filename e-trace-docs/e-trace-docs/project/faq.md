# FAQ

**Is this real money?** No. E-Trace runs on BNB Smart Chain Testnet. TLKM and tBNB are test tokens with no market value.

**Do I need a crypto wallet?** No. Sign up with your email and a PIN and E-Trace creates a wallet for you. If you already use MetaMask, you can log in with it instead.

**Where do I get TLKM?** Every new account gets 1,000 TLKM. You can also borrow TLKM through [Paylater](../user-flows/paylater.md).

**Can E-Trace take my money out of escrow?** No. Only the buyer can confirm or refund, and the arbiter can only choose between that item's buyer and seller. Nobody can send escrowed funds anywhere else. See [Escrow](../how-it-works/escrow.md).

**What if the seller never ships?** After 3 days you can refund yourself in full, and the keeper will also refund you automatically.

**What if I forget to confirm?** A few days after the parcel is delivered, the keeper releases the payment to the seller, so honest sellers aren't left waiting.

**How much does E-Trace charge?** Sellers pay 1% when a sale completes. Buyers pay nothing extra, refunds are free, and donations are 0%. See [Business model](business-model.md).

**How do I verify a transaction?** Every order page links to BscScan, and the public [Explorer](../how-it-works/explorer.md) shows marketplace-wide numbers. The contract addresses are listed on [Contract addresses](../smart-contracts/addresses.md).

**Is my personal data on-chain?** No. Names, addresses and phone numbers stay in the app database. The chain only sees wallet addresses, amounts and order IDs.
