# Seller

{% hint style="info" %}
During testnet, seller accounts are turned on by the E-Trace team. Ask us and we will switch your account to the seller role.
{% endhint %}

### 1. Set up your store

From the **Seller** dashboard, open **Store** and fill in your store name, description, origin address (used to calculate shipping), notification email, logo and banner. Your store gets a public page at `e-trace.shop/store/<your-store>`.

### 2. List products

Add products with a name, description, photos, stock and a price. Buyers see the price in TLKM; 1 TLKM is displayed as Rp1,000.

### 3. Ship orders

New paid orders appear under **Seller → Orders**. The money is already locked in escrow, so you know the buyer has paid before you ship. Mark the order as shipped and add tracking.

You have **3 days** to ship. After that the buyer can refund themselves, and the keeper can refund them automatically.

### 4. Get paid

When the buyer confirms, or the keeper completes the order after delivery, the escrow sends the money straight to your wallet:

```
you receive = item price − 1% platform fee
```

The fee is taken only when money is released. A refund returns the full amount to the buyer and costs you nothing.

### 5. Reports

**Seller → Reports** shows revenue, platform fees, VAT on those fees, and cost of goods for any period. Download it as Excel or PDF for your own bookkeeping.
