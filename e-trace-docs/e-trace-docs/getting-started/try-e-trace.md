# Try E-Trace in 5 minutes

E-Trace runs on BNB Smart Chain **Testnet**. Everything you spend is a test token, so you can click around freely.

### 1. Create an account

Open [e-trace.shop](https://e-trace.shop) and pick one of two ways in:

* **Email.** Register with your email, enter the one-time code we send you, then set a 6-digit **PIN**. E-Trace creates an embedded wallet for you. You never see a seed phrase; the PIN signs your transactions.
* **Browser wallet.** Connect MetaMask (or any injected wallet) on BNB Smart Chain Testnet and sign a login message.

Both login forms are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. You can turn on 2FA later from your profile.

### 2. Get test tokens

* **TLKM:** every new account receives **1,000 TLKM** from the platform wallet, so you can shop right away.
* **Gas (tBNB):** embedded wallets are topped up with gas automatically before a PIN transaction. If you use MetaMask, get tBNB from the [BNB Chain testnet faucet](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/testnet-faucet).

Your balance is shown on the **Wallet** page and is read straight from the chain.

### 3. Buy something

1. Browse products and add one or more to your cart. Items from different sellers can share one cart.
2. Go to checkout, pick a shipping address, and pay in TLKM.
3. Open the transaction on [BscScan Testnet](https://testnet.bscscan.com). You will see your TLKM go into the escrow contract, not to the seller.

### 4. Finish the order

When the seller ships and the parcel arrives, press **Confirm received**. Only then does the escrow release the money to the seller, minus the 1% platform fee.

If the seller never ships, you get a full refund. See [Buyer flow](../user-flows/buyer.md) for every path.

### 5. Look around

* [**Explorer**](../how-it-works/explorer.md): public numbers for the whole marketplace.
* [**Paylater**](../user-flows/paylater.md): borrow TLKM against tBNB, or fund the pool and earn a share of the interest.
* [**Donate**](../user-flows/donate.md): give to a campaign and watch the disbursement on-chain.
* **Language:** switch between English and Bahasa Indonesia with the language switcher.
